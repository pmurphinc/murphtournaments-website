Podcast Shorts Studio

A personal tool that turns footage you already hold into short vertical videos, and publishes them to your own channels on a schedule.

It runs on one computer — the owner's. There is no hosted service and no account to create. It was built for one person to run their own channels, and that is all it does.

How it works

  1. Point it at footage you have the right to use — a folder of clips, or a shared folder from the creator who made them.
  2. It transcribes each clip locally and renders a vertical version, with captions burned in where they belong.
  3. You review what it proposes and approve or reject it, with a reason. Nothing is published that you have not seen.
  4. Approved clips go out on a daily schedule to the channels you connected.
  5. It reads back views, likes and comments so you can see how each one did.

What it will not do

  • Download video from YouTube, TikTok or any platform through unofficial means.
  • Publish anything you have not authorised, from a source whose rights you have not recorded.
  • Send your footage, transcripts or credentials anywhere except the platform you are publishing to.
  • Claim a licence to share clips is also a licence to monetise them. Those are tracked separately and never conflated.
Every clip is tied to a recorded authorisation before it can be processed, and again before it can be published. A licence that lapses stops production, not just posting.

Connected accounts

It connects to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok only when you authorise it, and only to publish to accounts you own. Access tokens are encrypted on your own computer. You can disconnect any account at any time, which revokes the grant and deletes the stored token.
